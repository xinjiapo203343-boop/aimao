How Escrow Payment Works

Like Taobao's buyer protection — your money is held safely until the service is delivered and confirmed.

1

Select a Provider

Browse verified secretaries, nominee directors, or service packages and send an engagement request.

2

Fund Escrow

Pay via card, bank transfer, or crypto. Funds are held securely — the provider cannot access them yet.

3

Service Delivered

The provider fulfils the engagement. You review documents and confirm everything is complete.

4

Funds Released

On your confirmation, payment is instantly released to the provider. Dispute? Our team mediates within 48 hrs.
